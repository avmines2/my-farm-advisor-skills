1. Dataset scope and locations

The analysis covers one generated farm for each grower: 10 fields in Illinois, 10 in Iowa, and 10 in Nebraska. The fields total approximately 16,193.4 acres. The field inventory places the Illinois fields in Vermilion County with one field in Edgar County; Iowa fields span Boone, Hamilton, and Story Counties; Nebraska fields are in York County.

The three data layers are: (1) USDA Cropland Data Layer field boundaries, represented as a 2023 boundary snapshot; (2) NASA POWER daily weather sampled at field centroids and summarized for April–September in 2021–2025; and (3) USDA CDL field composition history for 2019–2023.

2. Comparison design

Comparison levels

  • Field: acreage, polygon compactness, crop mix, and field identifiers.
  • Field-year: annual weather summaries and CDL crop shares/classes.
  • Within grower: dispersion across the ten fields in each state.
  • Across growers: descriptive comparison of Illinois, Iowa, and Nebraska.

What the artifact set contains

  • Six statistical category figures: two each for boundaries, weather, and CDL/cropland.
  • Three CSV comparison analyses: boundary, weather, and CDL.
  • One three-panel static geospatial map.
  • Reusable per-grower tables and cross-grower rollups remain in the external runtime; this committed HTML is the readable submission artifact.

6. Geospatial map

The map presents the three field sets in separate panels, with polygons colored by the 2023 CDL crop snapshot and field IDs labeled. It is intended to show field-boundary geometry, crop labeling, and the spatial organization of fields within each grower—not statewide relative position, sensor coverage, or management history.

7. Limitations, assumptions, and scope boundaries

  • Boundaries are a single 2023 snapshot and do not provide a boundary time series.
  • Weather is NASA POWER centroid/grid data, not in-field sensor data. Effective grid-cell counts are IL 2, IA 2, and NE 1.
  • The 2019–2023 CDL history is classification/composition data, not a verified management record.
  • Crop shares retain filtered background/No Data residuals; estimated acres are boundary acres multiplied by reported shares.
  • Only ten fields per grower are available, so comparisons are descriptive and not inferential.
  • No p-values, causal claims, yield analysis, irrigation conclusions, or weather–crop correlation is made.
  • Soil analysis was not required, was not the focus, and was not read or analyzed in this EDA workflow.
  • Satellite, terrain, and other unrequested layers were also outside scope.
